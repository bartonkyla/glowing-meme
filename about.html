About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

We are a community nonprofit built by neighbors who wanted a hopeful, honest path from street or shelter overflow to a lasting home.

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2018 when a small group of Minneapolis and Saint Paul fosters kept hearing the same request: “Can you take one more?” Municipal shelters and partner clinics were full, and animals with treatable medical needs were waiting the longest.

We formed as a foster-based rescue so pets could heal in homes, not kennels.Our first year we placed 41 animals. As of 2026, coordinators now work from an office near Lake Street and still rely on volunteer drivers, weekend adoption tables, and families who open a spare room.

We are not a municipal shelter and we are not related to other Twin Cities rescue’s branding or programs. Our purpose is to raise awareness and provide a clear message: If you can foster, volunteer, adopt, or partner, we will put you to work with training and support from our team.

Core Values

These values guide intake decisions, adopter conversations, and how we talk about animals who are still waiting.

Compassion First

Every dog and cat arrives with a history. We treat medical needs, behavioral concerns, and never rush pets into a home that is not ready.

Honest Matches

We describe energy level, housetraining, and special care in plain language. A trustworthy match lasts longer than a fast placement.

Community Action

Rescue is local work. Volunteers, donors, clinics, and foster homes share the load so one household never carries an animal alone.

Partner Acknowledgments

We could not keep animals in care without the clinics, trainers, and neighborhood businesses that donate time and supplies. We thank:

  • Midway Companion Clinic in Saint Paul, for discounted spay, neuter, and dental days
  • North Loop Walk Club, for weekend socialization walks with anxious dogs
  • Powderhorn Pet Supply Co-op, for food, litter, and crate donations
  • Riverside Community Foundation, for microchip and vaccine grants
