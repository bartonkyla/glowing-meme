Service Area

We place animals and support foster homes in Minneapolis, Saint Paul, and communities within about 60 miles, including Bloomington, Richfield, Roseville, Maplewood, Minnetonka, and Stillwater.

Transport volunteers may live a little farther out if they can reach a clinic or adoption event. We cannot place fosters who have no way to reach a Twin Cities veterinary partner.

Office Hours

Coordinators work from our Lake Street office and answer interest forms during these hours. The form can be sent any time.

  • Tuesday through Friday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
  • Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. (adoption event weeks only)
  • Sunday and Monday: closed for foster check-ins by appointment
